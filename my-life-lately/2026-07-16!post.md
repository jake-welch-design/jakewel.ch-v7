I love this part of summer, where the rush of early warm-weather plans slow, and life becomes more relaxed and care-free. The fireflies start to come out at dusk, and our free days are spent swimming in the ocean and lounging on the beach with friends or picnicking in the park with take-out and ice cream.

This Sunday is the World Cup final with Argentina and Spain, and I'm torn on who I want to take it. Argentina feels so scrappy this year (reminiscent of the Knicks, some have said), but they won last year, so I don't know. To be honest, the last 7 or 8 teams I've rooted for have lost, so I don't have much of an opinion on it. This has been my favorite World Cup in memory, mainly because it's been such a community affair here in NYC. Just like the NBA playoffs, I've loved seeing it bring people together and be something to look forward to on weekday evenings.
