Well everyone, this is my last message. I'm sending this post with the last 1% of my phone battery as I drift out into the ocean. The Nor'easter storm that every news station in the country foretold in horror hit NYC with unprecedented force. You can think of it like a hurricane Sandy 2.0. The ocean levels rose into the streets, completely inundating every last brick and rat. The receding waters have ripped me out to sea, stray pizza boxes and sacks of garbage the only buoys to keep me afloat. Goodbye. Please remember me.
<br><br>
Of course, the Nor'easter was just a regular old storm with a bit of wind. It always makes me laugh how over-stated weather events can be in NYC. Not to say parts of the city didn't experience the storm worse than others, but for the most part, it wasn't all that different from other storms we've had this year. And it didn't stop Steph and I from getting out, either! Over the weekend, we stayed busy meeting some new friends out for drinks, running errands around the city, seeing a choral concert in a gorgeous church, and going out dancing. Rain be damned! I can't believe how quickly autumn was dropped into our laps. A couple weeks ago we were sweating it out and lounging in parks. Now we'll huddled inside with the heated blanket turned on. Now the trees just need to change colors to catch up!
<br><br>
This past weekend, we attended a lovely wedding for some dear friends in Connecticut on the coast, and it was absolutely gorgeous. Their ceremony backdrop was the sea, and we had a wonderful evening dancing, celebrating their love, and meeting all of their close friends and family. It was so special to be there! This was the first wedding we've attended in years, and it reminded us how fun it is to have an excuse to dress up nice and celebrate people you care about. I also have to point out that Steph made, that's right, designed and sewed her dress, and I think it turned out beautifully. I'm very proud of her!
