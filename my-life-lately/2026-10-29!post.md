Checking in, in peak autumn. The trees are bright orange and red, and the weather hasn't been so perfect all year. It's been a busy month for Steph and me! After a streak of doing nothing around here, we've been in full host mode for the past couple of weeks (and I've been sick between guests!). Bleck!) Mom visited mid-month, and Brayden and Kat were here just last weekend. It's been exhausting but so much fun! We love having the excuse to play tourist in our own city, to do all the things we never do when it's just us two. 

Some of the things we've gotten to experience with our guests: 
- We drove to Philly with my mom, a first for all of us, and we saw some cool early American historical sites 
- I visited The Frick for the first time
- We walked the Brooklyn Bridge for the first time since Steph was an intern here in 2019
- We saw Hadestown with Kat and Brayden on Broadway, which was fantastic 
- Brayden and I went to the New York Transit Museum, totally underrated
I'm sure I'm missing some things, but we've loved having everyone here. 

For October, I've been really enjoying watching more movies than usual. I wanted to get into the cozy autumn spirit and spooky energy of Halloween, so I've been watching classic horror and mystery films. Some favorites have been Dial M for Murder by Hitchcock, Robert Eggers' Nosferatu, Rosemary's Baby, and Mulholland Drive. Steph and I have also been enjoying movies in theaters. We recently saw Prime Time and You Can See Everything (which both happen to be directed by Lance Oppenheim!), and they were both phenomenal. Movies used to be an important interest to me, but when we sold our TV to be more present and de-center TV from our living space a few years ago, that fated into the background. It's been fun to bring that back again.

Those who know me know I’ve become a huge fan of [NTS](https://nts.live) this year. Well, to expand on my love for radio, I’ve also recently discovered [radio.garden](https://radio.garden), which connects to actual radio stations around the globe. I’ve been listening to stations I used to listen to in Utah and Colombia, local stations in New York like 8 Ball Radio, and I’ve discovered some other great stations in places like South Africa, like The Pan American Space Station. It’s amazing to bring this method of listening to music back into my life, and it has awakened an itch to become a DJ again, like in my old high school days with KPGR. Who knows, maybe I’ll volunteer somewhere?
