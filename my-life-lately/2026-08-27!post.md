My next project that's in the works involves <a href="https://www.nyc.gov/content/planning/pages/our-work/plans/citywide/privately-owned-public-spaces">POPS</a>, Privately Owned Public Spaces around NYC. These are "areas dedicated for public use and enjoyment, owned and maintained by private property owners in exchange for bonus floor area or zoning waivers." I've known about these spaces for a while now, but recently there's been a lot of buzz around them because of instances where properly owners have actively (and illegally) discouraged their use, or tried to make them hard to find or enjoy, like by putting up ropes to block access, or turning on sprinklers near benches 24/7. I've gone down a massive rabbit hole and have found that they're not only hard to find, but that their documentation is spotty. The official NYC city map for them is obviously not designed to be used for average NYers. Other resources exist, but they're all incredibly outdated, broken, or lacking in usability. Since the data is public, I've decided to build my own tool, one that not only allows for a better user experience, but also allows for community submissions to survey and report on their condition and share useful information like updated hours, photos, if a space offers WIFI, etc. Let me know if you have any ideas!
