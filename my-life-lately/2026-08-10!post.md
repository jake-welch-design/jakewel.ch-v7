Hi everyone, it's been about a month since I last updated you on what's going on in <a href="https://jakewel.ch/my-life-lately/">my-life-lately</a>. It's been very, very stormy, significantly more so than last summer. It's actually been a cause for concern in the city. Luckily, living on a hill, we don't deal with it much, but just about every rainstorm we've had, and that's multiple a week, has caused widespread flash flooding across the city. As the weather grows more extreme due to climate change, our infrastructure simply isn't equipped to handle it. Life for us, however, has been good, if not a bit cooped up. We got a projector set up just in time, and we have been watching plenty of movies on the rainy days. Poor Pepper has been just as cooped up as we've been, so we've been having more porch hangs together. Sunny days have been spent at the park or at the beach, as usual. 
<br><br>
A few weeks ago we discovered an ice cream place near us called Uncle Louie G's, and wow... it's so good. They have these things called Ice Swirls that are insane; we can't get enough of them. Imagine this: a base of extremely creamy, decadent vanilla soft serve ice cream, a scoop of Italian ice in your choice of flavor, topped with another swirl of soft serve. When we found this place, we told so many of our friends that we accidentally booked ourselves to go three times in one week, each time with someone new... by the end of the week we were so sugared out lol. 
<br><br>
My creativity has been in a strange rut these past couple months. I've felt dissatisfied with the books I've been reading, and I haven't had almost any brain power for pursuing my own ideas. I haven't felt very inspired at all, really; for the most part I've been keeping my head down and working. Luckily, work has been going very well, and I've been working on some cool projects with great clients building things I genuinely enjoy making. That said, it's been months since I made anything for myself. Luckily, this past week or so, I've felt this cloud dissipating. On Friday, I saw <a href="https://www.are.na/block/38315738">this image</a> on are.na and was immediately drawn to it. I liked how it was reminiscent of a Xerox scan or some figure drawing that you might find in an old art book. I liked the subject matter of some kind of medieval pilgrim pin. I loved how the format made it almost resemble stone. The spark this image kindled grew into an inextinguishable fire, and I spent the next few hours building this new <a href="https://jake-welch-design.github.io/stone_carver_tool/">stone carver tool</a> that converts images into warped stone-like sculptures by blending various depth maps and filters. You can see some images I've made with it in <a href="https://jakewel.ch/some-of-my-work/">some-of-my-work/</a>. It's been exhilarating to make something like this again, and the positive response I've received from sharing it has been a huge boost. I try not to put too much weight in online metrics and the opinions of others, but it's impossible not to feel good when something you make is appreciated by others. 
<br><br>
This cloud of uninspiration(?) has also lifted when it comes to reading. I've been reading Moby Dick, and it's been so much fun. I've been surprised by how humorous it is, and I love the freedom in the format Melville chose to write. One chapter might be silly narrative, while another is dreamlike, almost like poetry. Another might feel like reading facts from a textbook, and the next might feel like a story told in a tavern with a group of sailors. But it's always pushing the story forward, building up a legend, and it's constantly interesting and entertaining. There's no wonder it's been a classic novel for nearly 200 years. I've also been writing in a small notebook at the park again, and I've found it helps me clear my mind and notice small details in things happening around me. I haven't been writing much this summer, and maybe that's contributed to the rut I've found myself in. It's very grounding.
<br><br>
Anyway, I want to encourage you reading this to out and make something. Maybe try writing if you're feeling uninspired; just write down what you see and feel in real time, stream of consciousness. Flip through some old books on a subject you find interesting. Follow through on an idea, even if it's a small one. It might grow into something great!
