Hi, it's been a while! I have lots of images to share today but not a lot to say. Today is Independence Day and America's 250th birthday. Tonight we're going with our friends to the Brooklyn Bridge to see the huge firework show. Add it to the list of exciting things that have happened in the past month. Where to even begin? Since summer started, we've been go go go! NYC is the funnest place on Earth in the summertime, and this year might be one of the best NYC summers in history (heatwave be damned!). Folks have been joking that "if last year was Euro summer, this year is New York summer." The Knicks won the NBA Championship, the World Cup has been in full swing, and every week there's a new reason to hang with our neighbors and take to the street celebrating. I've waited in lines for limited edition city soccer jerseys, watched basketball on TV's set up on the sidewalk, ate Ukrainian food at the beach, seen comedy shows, seen one of our favorite musicians (Rosalia) in concert, attended Chess the musical on Broadway, and so on. Most recently, we've returned from a week long vacation away from the city with friends on a house boat in Lake Powell, and then a quick stop in Utah to meet our brand new niece. Life is good!
<br><br>
I was trying to think about what to write... find something inspiring or thoughtful to say... but honestly, I don't feel like I've had a lot on my mind lately. I realized I've been too busy living life to sit around musing. That's what summer should be all about! Getting out of the house and experiencing new things. During our week in Lake Powell, I barely took any photos. I never had my phone near. We didn't even pay attention to time, just wake up, swam, played, chatted. So here's to staying away from our phones and getting out to live life! I think my photos I'm sharing this time around will tell a better story than words will anyway, so with that, I'll stop my yapping. I hope everyone has a fun and safe holiday weekend!
